Welcome

A Home Away From Home

Urban Retreat is Mangalore's first-ever homestay, a modern bungalow designed in harmony with the land using traditional laterite stones and terracotta tiles. Nestled in a pollution-free environment near Pilikula Nisargadhama, we offer 6 elegantly furnished rooms that blend simple luxury with the warmth of home.

Whether you're visiting for a family vacation, medical tourism, or a corporate retreat, our friendly staff and personalized service ensure a memorable stay on the beautiful coast of Karnataka.
