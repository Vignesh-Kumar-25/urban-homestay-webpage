Directions

How to Reach Us

Mangalore is well connected by air, rail, and road. Our homestay is conveniently located near Pilikula Nisargadhama, Vamanjoor.

By Air

Mangalore International Airport (Bajpe) is approximately 20 km from the city. Taxis and cabs are available from the airport.

By Train

Mangalore Central and Mangalore Junction railway stations connect to major cities across India. Auto-rickshaws and taxis are available.

By Road

Mangalore is well connected via NH-75 and NH-66. KSRTC and private buses operate from the KSRTC bus stand in the city centre.
