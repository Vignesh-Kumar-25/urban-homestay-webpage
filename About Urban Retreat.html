Our Story

Mangalore's First Homestay

Urban Retreat is Mangalore's first-ever homestay, established in 2008. A modern bungalow designed in harmony with the land and terrain, our property is built using traditional laterite stones and terracotta tiles that reflect the rich architectural heritage of coastal Karnataka.

Nestled in a pollution-free environment near Pilikula Nisargadhama — one of the region's most treasured ecological preserves — Urban Retreat offers 6 elegantly furnished rooms that blend simple luxury with the warmth of home.

Whether you arrive as a guest, you leave as family. Our personalized service, friendly staff, and attention to detail create an experience that goes beyond a typical hotel stay. Here, you'll find the comfort of home with the convenience of professional hospitality.

“Touch of Home, Simple Luxury”

An unspoiled place where city lights don't mask the blue skies. A home, miles away from your own home — where family atmosphere meets professional hospitality.
